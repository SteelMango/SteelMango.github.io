Challenge 11. Name that Street! on Bellingcat Challenge, found at this link [https://challenge.bellingcat.com/challenge/11](https://challenge.bellingcat.com/challenge/11). In this challenge we need to find out the street name of the photo to complete the challenge.

1. First, I Expanded the photo to full screen to see for tiny details such as the reg plates on the cars and the language on the posts.

![reg plate](/assets/images/EU%20country.jpg)

2. In Europe the first two letters of the reg plate tell you where the car is from, known as the area code. Let’s look at the black Merc and find its area code.. 

> OF LC 471 
> OF is a city in Germany, Offenbach am Main.

Before I assume that the country is Germany, let’s look at other reg plates and signs around the area.
> 1. The white land rover evoque has a reg plate with WIaz226 with an area code of WI.
> 2. WI code is also in Germany, Wiesbaden.

![germany plates](/assets/images/germanyplates.jpeg)

3. Lets assume that the country is in Germany.
![germany country](/assets/images/DENMARK.webp)

4. Lets try to find specifically where in Germany this street is located. Looking at the shop near the blue umbrella, it reads a name JP Stoffee, a shop that sells fabrics. There are currently only three main JP Stoffee shops in Germany on Google Maps

![jpstoffee](/assets/images/JP%20STOFPE.png)

5. Lets go into street view each shop and see if the street layout matches with the photo.

![shop stoffe](/assets/images/shop1.png)

Even with the redesign of the shops logo the surroundings remain the same such as the shops surround it. The trees leaves have also off.

![shop 2](/assets/images/shop2.png)

6. Now looking at the street views street location we can see that the street name of the photo is Stiftstraße.

![location](/assets/images/locationplace.png)
