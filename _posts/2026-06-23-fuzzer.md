## First off, What is a Fuzzer and Why is it Relevant?
A Fuzzer is like a dog and a tunnel. You tell the dog to search the tunnel and the dog comes back showing you the possible entrances the tunnel leads to but not only just the where the entrances are but what type of entrances they are made out of like wood, rock and stone.

![Results from Glyphid](/assets/images/results1.png)

 Cyber security wise, these entrances are hidden websites that the website owner didn't intend to leave open for the publics eyes or a snooping attacker.
